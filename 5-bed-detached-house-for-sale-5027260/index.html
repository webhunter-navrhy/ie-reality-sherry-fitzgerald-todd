Description

Sherry FitzGerald Todd are delighted to bring to the market this Architecturally Designed 5-Bedroom A0-Rated Bungalow in a tranquil rural setting Lavally, Tuam, Co. Galway.

This brand-new A0-Rated, architecturally designed bungalow offers the perfect blend of modern comfort and peaceful countryside living. Situated in the serene townland of Lavally, just outside Tuam town in north County Galway, the property occupies a large, private site surrounded by picturesque open farmland and full uninterrupted views of Lavally Lake.

Finished to an exceptional standard, this spacious 5-bedroom, 4-bathroom home is freshly completed and ready for its first occupants. The striking exterior combines crisp white render with charcoal grey windows creating a sleek, contemporary aesthetic. Mature hedgerows frame the site, providing both privacy and scenic rural views.

Inside, the home is presented to a high-spec builder's finish—an ideal blank canvas for new owners to personalise. Natural light floods the interior through large windows, particularly in the expansive open-plan kitchen/living/dining area, which boasts corner window looking directly out on to the Lake. The thoughtfully designed layout includes four en-suite bedrooms, making it ideal for families or hosting guests in comfort. The open plan living, kitchen and dining has great flow. Upstairs has hollowcore flooring installed and there are heating controls installed throughout the house.

Every system and service has been expertly installed for modern, energy-efficient living. At the heart of the home is a meticulously arranged plant room, fitted with a Firebird Air to water system, hot water cylinder, a mechanical ventilation with heat recovery (MVHR) system for fresh air circulation, and zoned underfloor heating operated via a smart manifold system. A large pressure vessel and expansion tanks ensure stable water pressure and temperature throughout. All copper piping is neatly installed and clearly labelled—showcasing not only the functionality but the build quality and attention to detail this home offers. ( qualifies for green mortgage. )

Whether you're seeking a peaceful family retreat or a stylish countryside residence with cutting-edge comfort, this exceptional property delivers on every level. Option to work with the builder on new kitchen finish. A0-Rated energy rating. Construction is due to be completed in the next 6-8 weeks.
